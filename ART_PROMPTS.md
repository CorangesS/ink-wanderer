Bottom right: a floating elongated forest ghost in smoky sage black robes, a small pale cream triangular wooden mask with red eyes, branch-like antlers, holding a tiny vermilion glowing ink orb. Entire background transparent, no shadows, no floor, no border, no labels, no text. Square atlas.

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Both final assets are copied into this project's assets directory.
